Keys are read once, at startup. After rotating one in the Secret, roll the pod:
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout restart deploy/{{ include "ivanti-mcp.fullname" . }}
or change rolloutToken in your values. A rollout ends every open session, since
sessions live in this one pod's memory; clients reconnect with a new one.

One release is one tenant and one audience. Another tenant, or the other
audience, is another release.
